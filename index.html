// PhD Student · Emory University

I am a PhD student in Computer Science at Emory University, co-advised by Prof. Li Xiong and Prof. Fei Liu. Before Emory, I received my M.S. in Computer Science from UMass Amherst, advised by Prof. Hong Yu, and my B.S. in Computer Science (Honors Program) from Penn State, with minors in Mathematics, Statistics, and Cybersecurity. In summer 2026, I was a GenAI Research Intern at Zoom, where I built Zoom's next-generation agentic personalized meeting summarization and studied harness design for coding agents.

My research centers on LLM agents: how they plan under cost constraints, how harness design shapes the behavior of coding agents, and how interactive AI systems can elicit what people actually need. Earlier, I worked on clinical NLP for patient education and public-health surveillance.
