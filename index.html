About Me

I am a PhD student in the Department of Computer Science at Emory University, fortunate to be advised by Prof. Li Xiong. Previously, I was fortunate to be advised by Dr. Fei Liu. Before Emory, I received my M.S. in Computer Science from UMass Amherst, advised by Prof. Hong Yu, and my B.S. in Computer Science (Honors Program) from Penn State University, with minors in Mathematics, Statistics, and Cybersecurity.

Research Interests: My primary research interests include large language models, LLM agents, and natural language processing. Specifically, my current research focuses on:

  • LLM Agents and Planning: how agents plan under cost constraints, tree-search planning, and evaluating the planning capabilities of LLMs.
  • Coding Agents: harness design, context management, and failure analysis for long-horizon software-engineering agents.
  • LLM Safety & Privacy: privacy risks and safeguards for LLMs and agentic systems, and building trustworthy agents that act safely on users' behalf.

I am happy to collaborate and/or answer questions about my research. If you are interested in research collaboration or have any inquiries about my experience, please send me an email.
